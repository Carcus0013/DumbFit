Barbell Shrug - Front View

Barbell Shrug - Side View

Exercise Instructions

  1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding the barbell with both hands slightly wider than shoulder-width.
  2. Bend forward slightly at the hips, knees slightly bent, keeping your back straight.
  3. Engage your traps and shoulder blades—imagine trying to touch them together.
  4. Hold briefly at the top and slowly release the shrug.
  5. Repeat these steps 3-5 sets of 15 reps
