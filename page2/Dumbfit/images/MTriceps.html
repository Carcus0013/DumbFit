Tricep Exercises

Cable Rope Pushdowns - Front View

Cable Rope Pushdowns - Side View

Exercise Instructions

  1. The cable should be set all the way at the top of the machine.
  2. Make sure to keep your upper arm glued at your side.
  3. Extend your elbows until you feel your triceps contract.
  4. Repeat these steps 3-5 sets of 15 reps
