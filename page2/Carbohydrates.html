Your Guide to High-Carbohydrate Foods

Carbohydrates are one of the three main macronutrients along with proteins and fats. They serve as the body's primary energy source, providing fuel for your brain, muscles, and other vital organs. This guide highlights foods that are particularly high in carbohydrates per 100g serving, helping you make informed dietary choices.

Top High-Carbohydrate Foods

White Rice (Cooked)
28.2g per 100g

White rice is a refined grain that has had its bran, germ, and husk removed. It's a staple food for more than half the world's population and is particularly prominent in Asian cuisines.

Nutrient Amount
Calories 130 kcal
Protein 2.7g
Fiber 0.4g
White Bread
49.0g per 100g

White bread is made from wheat flour that has been refined to remove the bran and germ. This results in a softer, lighter bread with a longer shelf life but fewer nutrients than whole grain alternatives.

Nutrient Amount
Calories 265 kcal
Protein 9.0g
Fiber 2.7g
Potatoes (Boiled)
20.1g per 100g

Potatoes are starchy tubers that are a staple food in many countries. They're particularly rich in potassium and vitamin C, while providing a significant source of carbohydrates.

Nutrient Amount
Calories 87 kcal
Protein 1.9g
Fiber 1.8g
Pasta (Cooked)
30.9g per 100g

Pasta is typically made from durum wheat semolina or flour. It's a versatile food that forms the base of many Mediterranean dishes and provides a good source of complex carbohydrates.

Nutrient Amount
Calories 158 kcal
Protein 5.8g
Fiber 1.8g
Honey
82.4g per 100g

Honey is a sweet, viscous food substance produced by bees. It consists primarily of simple sugars, predominantly fructose and glucose, making it very high in carbohydrates.

Nutrient Amount
Calories 304 kcal
Protein 0.3g
Fiber 0.2g
Bananas
22.8g per 100g

Bananas are a widely consumed fruit that contains significant amounts of potassium, vitamin B6, and vitamin C. They're particularly known for their carbohydrate content, which increases as they ripen.

Nutrient Amount
Calories 89 kcal
Protein 1.1g
Fiber 2.6g
Oats (Raw)
66.3g per 100g

Oats are a whole grain cereal known for their high fiber content, particularly beta-glucans. They provide a rich source of complex carbohydrates and are popular as a breakfast food worldwide.

Nutrient Amount
Calories 389 kcal
Protein 16.9g
Fiber 10.6g
Sweet Potatoes
20.1g per 100g

Sweet potatoes are starchy root vegetables rich in beta-carotene, which gives them their orange color. They provide complex carbohydrates along with significant amounts of vitamin A and potassium.

Nutrient Amount
Calories 86 kcal
Protein 1.6g
Fiber 3.0g
Dried Dates
75.0g per 100g

Dates are the fruit of the date palm tree and are particularly high in natural sugars. They're a good source of fiber, potassium, and several antioxidants while being extremely rich in carbohydrates.

Nutrient Amount
Calories 282 kcal
Protein 2.5g
Fiber 8.0g
